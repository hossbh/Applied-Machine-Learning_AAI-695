Notebook 01 · Lab

How complex should the model be?

Noisy samples of a cubic, y = 2x + 11x² + 3x³, fitted with polynomials of order 1 to 8, as in the notebook. The error on the training data keeps falling with order; the error on fresh data from the same source shows where extra order stops helping.

Notebook 02 · Lab

Normal equation against gradient descent

The same straight line found three ways: in closed form, by batch gradient descent and by stochastic gradient descent, with an 80/20 train/test split. The notebook's data file is not in the repository, so this lab draws a sample shaped like it (y ≈ −3.90 + 1.19x, 97 points).

Notebook 05 · Lab

K-means clustering, one step at a time

The notebook's algorithm: pick K points as starting centroids, assign every point to its nearest centroid, move each centroid to the mean of its points, and repeat until nothing changes. Step through it, or let it run.

Points and centroids

Choosing K: the elbow

Inertia, the sum of squared distances to the nearest centroid, for K = 1 to 8 (best of five starts). The bend marks a good K.

On MNIST digits

Mini-Batch K-means on the 60,000 training images, each cluster labelled with its most common digit

More clusters capture more ways of writing each digit, so labelling accuracy rises with K.

Beyond the labs

Networks, trees and a CAD project

These notebooks need their datasets and trained models, so they are summarised here with the results they reached.

04 · NumPy

Neural network from scratch

A two-hidden-layer (128–64) network written in NumPy, with backpropagation, classifying handwritten digits.

97.6 % accuracy on the 10,000-image MNIST test set.
03 · scikit-learn

Decision trees and random forests

Titanic survival from class, sex, age and family aboard, with pruning and a random forest.

78 % test accuracy for both the pruned tree and the forest.
06 · Keras

Diabetes prediction

A dense neural network predicting diabetes onset from eight clinical measurements in the Pima Indians dataset.

64.9 % test accuracy.
07 · Project

Material of 3D CAD parts

Part-level features extracted from the Autodesk Fusion 360 Gallery assemblies, cleaned, and used to compare four classifiers.

Random forest is best: 81 % accuracy after tuning.
